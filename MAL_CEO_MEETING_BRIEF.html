Mal · CEO Meeting Brief

Prepared by: Manar El Amin  ·  Brand, Communications & Marketing  ·  June 2026
Key Takeaway
Mal is a licensed financial institution first, AI-native second, and Shariah-compliant third. We do not believe in user personas — we have targets, but we serve everyone. The only moat is user-base and distribution; everything else is replicable. 90% of revenue will come from lending post-licence. Every marketing activity must tie to a growth metric and contribute at least 20% to growth.
