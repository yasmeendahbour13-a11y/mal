Strategic Context — The Next 3 Months
The next 3 months are not about broad brand awareness. They are about building trust, proving product credibility, and converting hype into qualified waitlist and SME demand. Marketing's role is to create the narrative, proof points, channels, and moments that help Growth build the user base — while staying within regulatory and product-readiness guardrails.
Marketing Strategy
Build trust first (licensed bank), hype second (AI-native benefits made tangible), and qualified demand third (creators, events, PR) — to drive waitlist and SME pipeline growth while staying within regulatory guardrails.
