Positioning — How We Talk About Mal (Priority Order)
P0 — LEAD WITH THIS
Licensed Financial Institution
"Clearly a bank — a full bank with a license"
This is what separates Mal from every fintech. We are not a wrapper, not a prepaid card, not an EMI. We are a licensed bank. This is always the first thing we say.
▶
P1 — DIFFERENTIATOR
AI-Native Banking
"Open an account in 30 seconds — a new generation of banking"
AI is not a feature we added. The entire bank is built on it. This is what makes us a new-gen bank, not a legacy bank with a chatbot.
▶
P2 — CHERRY ON TOP
Islamic & Shariah-Compliant
"Ethical banking as the added value layer"
Shariah compliance is built in, not bolted on. It's the ethical foundation — but we lead with the bank, not the religion. This layer earns trust without narrowing the audience.
Global vs UAE: Mal (global, AI-native, USD/ACH) ≠ Mal Bank (UAE, CBUAE-licensed). CBUAE does not care about global Mal. AAS = primary POC for Public Affairs.
Moat: "Only moat is user-base; everything else is replicable." No unique claim text yet — must be quantitative, not generic. Study Brave's marketing.
Messaging rule: No generic claims. "Faster, nicer" = noise. Only flex real experiences. "Transferred in 2 seconds" not "we're fast." Be quantitative or stay quiet.
