FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this really collateral-free?

Yes. We fund you based on your admission and future earning potential — not property, savings, or a co-signer.

How much can I get funded?

Up to $100,000 in tuition, paid directly to your university, plus additional living-expense support for eligible second-year students.

Do I have to fund my entire tuition through Mal Ascend?

No — you can choose to fund a portion or all of it, matched to what you actually need.

When do I start repaying?

Repayments pause while you're studying, with a grace period after you graduate before instalments begin. Full terms are confirmed at your offer stage.

Which universities are eligible right now?

Our pilot cohort covers Harvard, Princeton, Yale, Columbia, UPenn (Wharton), Cornell, Dartmouth (Tuck), INSEAD (Fontainebleau & Singapore), and London Business School — Master's and MBA programmes only.

Is there a cost to join the waitlist?

No. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn't obligate you to anything.
